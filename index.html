Unblock Master – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how IJASS Business ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the Unblock Master mobile game ("the app"). We designed Unblock Master to work offline and to keep game progress on your device.

Information the app collects

Unblock Master does not collect, transmit, or share personal information with us or with third parties. The app does not require an account or sign-in. It does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and it does not request the Android internet permission.

Information stored on your device

The app stores game data locally on your device so that it can remember your campaign progress, unlocked levels, scores, daily challenge records, and settings such as sound, music, and vibration preferences. This information is not sent to us. You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Uninstalling the app may permanently remove that locally stored progress.

Permissions

The app may use the Android vibration permission when you enable haptic feedback. Vibration is optional and controlled by the in-app setting. The app does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. It has no account system, advertising, analytics, or data transmission features.

Emails to support

If you choose to contact us at ijassbusiness@gmail.com, we will receive the information you include in your email, such as your email address and message, and use it to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive personal information. Your email provider may process your message under its own privacy policy.

Data security and retention

Because the app does not send game data to our servers and we do not operate an account or game-data server for the app, we do not hold your locally stored game progress. Support emails are retained only as reasonably necessary to respond to and manage the request, or as required by law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or information practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published. If a future app version adds services that collect or share data, this policy and the app's Google Play disclosures will be updated before that version is released.
